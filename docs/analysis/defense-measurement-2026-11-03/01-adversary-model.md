## What this table changes

- The two actors the current roadmap addresses (1 and 3-via-XSS) are real, but controls for them
  only raise cost; they do not remove the credential or the API authority.
- Actors 2, 4, 5 and 6 are **not addressed at all** by the seam, the guard, the pin gate, or the SRI
  work. Their stopping controls are server-side or process-side.
- The highest-leverage unmeasured question is actor 2's: **does the server trust Bearer claims or
  client-supplied identity?** If it trusts the body, every subordinate agent is a potential master,
  and client-side XSS work is secondary.
- The highest-severity finding available without live testing is actor 6's: **no financial
  reconciliation exists to contradict an inside or tampered change** (item 3).
